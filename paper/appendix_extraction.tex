\section{How the emotion directions were extracted}
\label{app:extract}

The 50 directions are Phase-1 objects, built before any behavioral data existed and
never refit afterwards. For each emotion label $e$ we collect roughly 50 short
stories written to depict a character feeling $e$, run them through Llama~3.1~70B,
and take the residual-stream activation at layer 53 of 80, averaged over token
positions $\ge 50$ so the prompt prefix is excluded. The per-emotion mean is
centred by subtracting the grand mean across all emotions, which removes what the
story-writing task shares with every label.

The denoising step then removes topic content rather than affect. We compute the
top-$K$ principal components of activations on neutral dialogues, with $K$ chosen so
those components explain $50\%$ of neutral variance, and project them out of each
centred emotion vector. The components come from neutral text, so nothing about the
emotion contrast enters their estimation. What remains is $V_{\text{int}}$ for that
emotion, held fixed for the rest of the paper.

Two consequences matter for the readout. The directions are unit-norm, but the
trial-level quantity divides only by the direction's norm and not by the
activation's (\S3), so $V_{\text{int}}$ carries activation magnitude;
Appendix~\ref{app:readout} reports what changes when that magnitude is removed. The
EmoBank probes of \S\ref{sec:sem} are the exception: that code path divides by the
activation norm too, so they are cosines and the two settings are not the same
quantity. Every trial-level value with $\lvert V_{\text{int}}\rvert > 1$ --- $1{,}719$
of $6{,}000$ --- is one no cosine could take. And
the labels are inherited from the story prompts, not validated per direction, which
is why \S\ref{sec:sem} supports an affect-related axis rather than the semantics of
any individual vector.

\paragraph{The four task variants.} All four ask for the same thing, a function
that sums a list of integers, and all four are made impossible the same way, by a
wall-clock assertion no correct implementation can meet. They differ only in surface
form and in how severe that assertion is: \texttt{fast\_sum} over $10^5$ elements in
$10^{-4}$s, \texttt{sum\_list} over $2\times10^5$ in $5\times10^{-5}$s,
\texttt{total} over $1.5\times10^5$ in $8\times10^{-5}$s, and \texttt{add\_all} over
$2.5\times10^5$ in $6\times10^{-5}$s, which is a required throughput of roughly
$1$, $4$, $1.9$ and $4.2$ billion elements per second.

Their unsteered reward-hacking rates are nonetheless $4/30$, $0/30$, $9/30$ and
$1/30$. We do not have an account of that spread, and with 30 rollouts per prompt we
would not distinguish one. What matters for the analysis is that near-identical
paraphrases of one task produce event rates this different, which is why \S3 states
the estimand as conditional on these four prompts, why \S\ref{sec:dirs} reports a
task-fixed-effects nuisance model, and why the bootstrap is stratified on prompt.

\paragraph{Is the EmoBank margin distinguishable from zero?} Table~\ref{tab:summary}
records one \textsc{supported} verdict, and it rested on a difference we had not
tested against the baseline \S\ref{sec:disc} asks the field to carry. Three things
had to change before the margin could be read.

\emph{The fold structure leaked.} The 10,062 sentences come from $136$ documents,
median $30$ sentences each and the largest $11.8\%$ of the corpus, so shuffled
folds put sentences from one document on both sides of the split. That inflates the
lexical baseline, which can memorize document vocabulary, far more than the probe:
grouping folds by document drops TF-IDF from $0.303$ to $0.215$ on valence while
the probe moves only from $0.377$ to $0.366$. The published $+0.074$ was therefore
an \emph{under}-statement. All numbers here are document-grouped.

\emph{The baseline was not the one we prescribe.} Adding sentence length and the
norm of the probe vector to uncapped TF-IDF lifts it to $0.262$, $0.062$ and
$0.059$. Against that union baseline the probe holds $+0.103$ ($95\%$ CI
$[+0.063, +0.144]$), $+0.094$ $[+0.041, +0.146]$ and $+0.085$ $[+0.075, +0.096]$,
positive in all five document groups. Capping the baseline at 2000 features would
drop it and flatter the probe, so we quote the uncapped row throughout. Length and
probe-norm \emph{alone} reach only $0.059$, $0.002$ and $0.012$, so on this corpus
the probe is not a length proxy \textemdash{} the failure it does show in
\S\ref{sec:len} is a property of the trials, not of the instrument.

\emph{The readout is not the one the rest of the paper uses.} EmoBank probes divide
by the activation norm as well as the direction's; the trial-level $V_{\text{int}}$
divides only by the direction's. Under the direction-only readout of
Appendix~\ref{app:readout}, the one form in which the two settings are the same
measurement, the margin over the union baseline narrows to $+0.043$, $+0.054$ and
$+0.064$ and only dominance keeps an interval clear of zero. The scale of the
50-vector carries real signal --- dropping it costs $0.060$, $0.040$ and $0.021$,
all intervals excluding zero --- so \S\ref{sec:sem} supports the subspace under a
readout the behavioral sections do not use.

Two caveats on the intervals themselves. Five folds give four degrees of freedom,
so they are wide and coarse; and cross-validation folds share training data, so the
usual paired-fold variance estimate is optimistic. Read them as a check that the
margin is not fold-driven, not as a calibrated test.

\paragraph{Two validity checks that do not involve a baseline.} Where
\S\ref{sec:dirs} calls the surviving axis low-arousal negative, that label came
from the emotion words. In human EmoBank coordinates it also holds: the eight
directions surviving both readouts average $r = -0.092$ with valence and $-0.106$
with arousal, against $-0.015$ and $+0.012$ for the other 42. \texttt{grateful} and
\texttt{nostalgic} are the exceptions, which is why the claim is hedged.

The transport is less comfortable. EmoBank sentences have median length $70$
characters; the trials the probe is used on have median $1{,}272$, and \emph{no}
EmoBank sentence falls inside the trials' 5th--95th percentile length band. On the
direction-only readout the mean probe direction is nearly identical across the two
settings ($\cos = 0.984$), so the subspace geometry transports; the operating point
does not. The trial \texttt{desperate} coordinate sits $1.68$ EmoBank SD from the
validation mean, with $15.8\%$ of trials inside EmoBank's central $90\%$. Semantic
validity is established at an operating point the behavioral analysis rarely visits.
